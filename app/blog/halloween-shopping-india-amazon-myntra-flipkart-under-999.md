## 💀 Three Budget Halloween Rules (That Actually Work)

**Rule 1: Face paint > full costume, always.**
A ₹500 face paint setup looks more intentional than a ₹1,500 pre-packaged costume that everyone else is also wearing.

**Rule 2: Buy the accessory, wear your own clothes.**
Devil horns on a black outfit. Witch hat on a dark dress. Vampire cape on a blazer. You look like *you tried*, without trying too hard.

**Rule 3: Decor is the actual vibe.**
If you're hosting: spend ₹300 on string lights and ₹200 on pumpkin props. Nobody remembers whose costume was best. Everyone remembers the house that looked actually spooky.
