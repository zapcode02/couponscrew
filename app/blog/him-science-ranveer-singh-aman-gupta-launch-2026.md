# Ranveer Singh and Aman Gupta Just Launched H.I.M — A Men's Skincare Brand Built for the Indian Man

Indian men's grooming just got a major shake-up.

Today — November 17, 2026 — Bollywood actor Ranveer Singh and boAt co-founder Aman Gupta officially launched **H.I.M**, short for **Handsome Indian Man**, a men's personal care brand built from the ground up for Indian skin, Indian climate, and Indian biology.

This isn't a celebrity slapping their name on someone else's product. Ranveer and Aman have co-founded the brand, and both appear in the launch film together. The brand is backed by Aman Gupta's venture studio, OFF/BEAT Studios — the same outfit behind boAt's rise.

Here's everything that just dropped.

---

## What Is H.I.M?

H.I.M stands for **Handsome Indian Man**. The brand's premise is straightforward: existing grooming products were largely designed for Western skin types and climates. India has heat, humidity, hard water, heavy pollution, and intense UV — conditions that most international formulations weren't built for.

H.I.M claims to have developed its products specifically around Indian male skin research, with Indian men as test subjects across varied Indian conditions.

The brand's proprietary technology is called **IN·XT™** — described as a supramolecular delivery system designed to push active ingredients past the skin's surface, not just sit on top of it. It's featured across all eight products in the launch range.

## Who's Behind It

**Ranveer Singh** needs no introduction. What's different here is that he's not just the face of the brand — he's a co-founder. In the launch communication, Ranveer says he tested every formulation from the first samples through to the final versions.

**Aman Gupta** is the co-founder of boAt, the Indian audio and wearables brand that became one of the country's biggest D2C success stories. He runs OFF/BEAT Studios, which builds and backs new consumer brands. H.I.M is his bet on the men's grooming category.

The combination is deliberate: Ranveer brings cultural credibility and mass reach, Aman brings the D2C playbook that scaled boAt to where it is.

## Why This Launch Matters

India's men's grooming market has been growing fast — driven by younger consumers, rising urban skincare awareness, and a category that was historically underserved. The gap has been filled mostly by legacy brands (Himalaya, Garnier Men, Nivea Men) and a wave of new D2C players like The Man Company, Bombay Shaving Company, and mCaffeine.

H.I.M enters with three things most of that field doesn't have together:
- **A proprietary ingredient story** (IN·XT™)
- **India-specific formulation claims** (hard water shampoo is a good example — a real problem in Delhi, Jaipur, Ahmedabad)
- **Two co-founders with genuine pull** — one mass-cultural, one D2C-operational

Whether the product lives up to the claims is something the market will decide in the next few months. But as a launch, it's hard to ignore.
