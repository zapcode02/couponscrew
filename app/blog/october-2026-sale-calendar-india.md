# October 2026 Sale Calendar: Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Ajio, Nykaa and What Comes After

October 2026's three biggest sales start within two days of each other. **Amazon Great Indian Festival opens on 8 October**, **Myntra Big Fashion Festival also opens on 8 October**, and **Flipkart Big Billion Days opens to everyone on 9 October**, with early access a day earlier for eligible members. None of the three has announced an end date yet.

## Festival dates that change when you should buy

Sale dates tell you when prices drop. Festival dates tell you when you actually need the item in hand. This year the two calendars sit close together, so delivery time matters more than usual.

| Festival | Date | What people usually buy |
|---|---|---|
| Sharad Navratri | 11 to 19 Oct | Ethnic wear, puja items, fasting groceries |
| Dussehra | 20 Oct | Vehicles, appliances, new clothes |
| Karwa Chauth | 28 Oct | Sarees, suits, jewellery, mehendi and makeup |
| Dhanteras | 6 Nov | Gold, silver, utensils, appliances |
| Diwali | 8 Nov | Gifts, decor, lights, sweets, clothes |
| Bhai Dooj | 11 Nov | Gifts for siblings |
| Chhath Puja | 15 Nov | Puja items, travel home |

Diwali falls on 8 November in 2026, nearly three weeks later than in 2025. Last year Big Billion Days started on 23 September; this year the later Diwali has pushed the big sales into October. It also leaves a gap of almost a month between the first sale week and Diwali, which most platforms are likely to fill with a second round of Diwali offers.

## Week-by-week: what to buy and where

The calendar below links each week's sales to the purchases that make sense in that window.

### 3 to 7 October: build your cart and watch early deals

This is the week to prepare, not to spend. Two things are already live: Amazon's Early Deals, which started on 25 September, and Flipkart's Curtain Raiser, which opens selected products at BBD prices five days before the main sale.

Use these days to:

- Add the items you want to your wishlist on each platform and **write down today's price**. You will need it on sale day to tell a real price drop from a sale banner on the same price.
- Check which bank card gets the instant discount on each platform. Amazon has listed SBI cards; Flipkart has listed Axis Bank and ICICI Bank cards. Our [comparison of Big Billion Days and Amazon Great Indian Festival](https://www.couponscrew.com/blog/big-billion-days-vs-amazon-great-indian-festival) puts the offers side by side.
- Decide whether early access is worth it for you. If you are buying a phone or a popular size in a fashion brand, the first hours matter. For most other purchases, the main sale day is fine.

### 7 to 10 October: the main sale window

The three biggest sales overlap almost completely in these four days. Prices on popular phones, laptops and TVs are usually lowest in the opening days, when stock is fullest and the launch-day deals run.

| Beauty | Nykaa, Myntra, Amazon | Compare all three; Nykaa's own big sale is expected later |

### 21 October to 5 November: Karwa Chauth and the Diwali run-up

This is the gap between the first sale wave and Diwali. Expect second-round Diwali sales from Amazon, Flipkart and Myntra, though none has announced dates yet.

- **Karwa Chauth (28 October)** drives demand for sarees, suits, jewellery and beauty. Nykaa's Diwali sale is expected to start around late October, which makes it a good time for makeup and skincare.
- **Diwali gifting** starts in earnest after Dussehra. If you are buying gifts in bulk for family or office, order before 1 November to stay clear of the last-week delivery rush.
- **Home decor and lights** go fast in the final fortnight. Buy them earlier than you think you need to.

## How to tell a real sale price from a sale banner

A sale tag does not always mean a lower price. Some listings raise the price in the weeks before a sale and then show a large discount on the inflated figure.

1. **Note the current price this week.** A screenshot of your wishlist is enough.
2. **Compare the sale price with your note, not with the MRP.** The MRP is rarely what anyone pays.
3. **Check the final price after the bank discount and coupon.** Two listings with the same sale price can end up different once bank offers and caps apply.
4. **Check the seller and return policy.** A lower price from a seller with no return option is not always the better deal.

Amazon has said its AI shopping features this year include price history on product pages, which should make this check quicker. ## Frequently asked questions

### Which sale starts first in October 2026, Amazon or Flipkart?
Amazon starts first. Amazon Great Indian Festival opens on 8 October 2026, and Flipkart Big Billion Days opens to everyone on 9 October. Flipkart members with Plus, Black, VIP or a Flipkart credit card get early access on 8 October, so both are effectively open from the same day for those members.

### When does Myntra Big Fashion Festival 2026 start?
Myntra Big Fashion Festival 2026 starts on 8 October 2026. Insiders and VIP ticket holders are reported to get early access from 7 October. Myntra has not announced the end date or a bank partner yet.

### When do Amazon and Flipkart's October 2026 sales end?
Neither Amazon nor Flipkart has announced an end date as of 3 October 2026. Past editions have run for about a week to ten days, followed by separate Diwali offers. We will update this page when the end dates are announced.

### Is there another sale before Diwali 2026?
A second round of Diwali sales is likely, but none has been announced yet. Diwali falls on 8 November 2026, almost a month after the first sales open, and platforms usually run Diwali-specific offers in the final weeks before the festival.

### Is it better to buy in October or wait for Black Friday?
For phones, laptops, TVs and large appliances, the October sales usually have the strongest offers. For beauty, international fashion brands and some gadgets, Black Friday on 27 November is worth waiting for if the purchase is not needed for Diwali.
