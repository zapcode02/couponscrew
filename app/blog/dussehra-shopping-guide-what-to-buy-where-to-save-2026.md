## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Why is Dussehra considered an auspicious day to shop?**  
Dussehra (Vijayadashami) is one of the *sade teen muhurtas* — among the most auspicious timings of the year in the Hindu calendar for new beginnings and major purchases. Jewellery, vehicles, and electronics bought on this day are traditionally considered to carry positive energy into the household.

**When do Dussehra sales typically end?**  
Most platform-specific Dussehra sales wrap within two to three days after the festival. After that, focus shifts to pre-Diwali pricing, which tends to be slightly higher on popular items as demand spikes again.

**Is it smarter to buy ethnic wear on Dussehra or Diwali?**  
Dussehra is usually the better window. Discounts are deeper, stock is fuller, and there's no delivery crunch. By the time Diwali week arrives, popular sizes and styles in ethnic wear are often sold out on major platforms.

**Can I get same-day delivery on pooja items this Dussehra?**  
Yes — Blinkit and Zepto deliver essentials like diyas, camphor, puja oil, and incense sticks within minutes in most metro areas. For fresh flowers and sweets, FNP and local options also offer same-day slots.

**Are Dussehra gift hampers worth ordering online?**  
For curated hampers — sweets, chocolates, corporate gifts — online platforms offer wider selection and clearer pricing than physical stores during the festive rush. Ordering a few days before the festival avoids the last-minute delivery queue.
