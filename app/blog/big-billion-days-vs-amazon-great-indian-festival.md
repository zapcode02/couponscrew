Big Billion Days vs Amazon Great Indian Festival 2026: Dates, Bank Offers and Which Sale Is Better

Flipkart Big Billion Days 2026 opens to everyone on October 9, with early access for Flipkart Plus, VIP, Black and Flipkart credit card members from midnight on October 8. The Amazon Great Indian Festival sale opens to everyone on October 8, with Prime members getting in a day earlier on October 7. Your bank card is the biggest deciding factor: Flipkart has Axis Bank and ICICI Bank, Amazon has SBI.

This guide lines up both sales side by side, explains what each offer actually means at checkout, and tells you which sale suits which kind of buyer. It will be updated as live prices go up.

When does the Amazon Great Indian Festival sale start?

The Amazon Great Indian Festival 2026 opens to all shoppers on October 8, and Prime members get early access from October 7. That puts Amazon's general opening on the same day as Flipkart's member-only early access, which makes side-by-side price checks easy for anyone who has both apps.

How bank discounts really work

"Up to 10%" almost always comes with a cap per card and a minimum order value. On a phone priced near a lakh, the cap usually limits the discount to a fixed rupee amount, not the full percentage. Check the offer terms under the product price before paying, since the capped amount is what reaches you.

Two more things catch shoppers out every year:

Bank discounts often do not apply to orders paid partly with gift card balance or wallet money.
EMI discounts on credit cards can be reversed if the EMI is converted or cancelled later.
Early access: Amazon Prime vs Flipkart Plus, VIP and Black

Early access gives members roughly a day to buy before stock thins out. Prime members start on October 7 and Flipkart's eligible members start at midnight on October 8.

Whether a membership is worth paying for depends on one thing: are you buying a limited-stock item on day one? For a flagship phone at a teased price, early access can be the difference between getting the deal and seeing "sold out". For everyday shopping like clothes or kitchen items, stock usually lasts, and the general sale works fine.

If you already have Prime for video or delivery, you get early access with no extra cost. Flipkart Plus is earned through SuperCoins from regular orders, while VIP and Black are paid tiers, so check your account page to see which tier you are on before the sale opens.

Amazon Business: GST invoices for business buyers

Amazon Business is marketing the Great Indian Festival to registered businesses as well. The main advantage is a GST invoice, which lets a GST-registered buyer claim input tax credit on eligible business purchases, along with bulk-order discounts on select items.

This matters if you are buying laptops, printers or office furniture for a registered business. Personal buyers cannot claim input tax credit, so the GST invoice brings no saving for household purchases. Speak to your accountant before treating any purchase as a business expense.

How to get the best price in either sale

The best deals go to shoppers who prepare a few days ahead. These steps take little time and save the most money:

Shortlist products now. Add them to your wishlist or cart on both apps, so you can compare quickly when prices change.
Note today's price. Sale-day "discounts" are sometimes measured from an inflated MRP. Knowing the current price tells you whether the sale price is a real drop.
Save your card and address. Checkout speed matters for limited-stock deals.
Read the bank offer cap. Check the maximum discount per card and the minimum order value.
Check the exchange estimate early. Enter your old device details before the sale to see a realistic value.
Look at the seller and warranty. For electronics, make sure the product carries an official brand warranty.

A note on Pay Later, EMI and loan offers

Both platforms push credit options hard during the sale. Flipkart's sale page lists a flat discount on Flipkart EMI purchases and on Flipkart Pay Later, and Amazon offers No Cost EMI on eligible items.

No Cost EMI usually means the interest is adjusted as a discount on the product price, but processing fees and GST on interest can still apply depending on the lender. Pay Later and pre-approved loans are credit products that affect your credit history if payments are missed.

This section is for information only and is not financial advice. Read the lender's terms, fees and repayment schedule before choosing any EMI, Pay Later or loan option.
