## 🪔 Dhanteras — 6 November 2026

### *"Buy something metal today. Your future self will thank you."*

Dhanteras is the one day of the year when buying is not just encouraged — it is considered auspicious. Goddess Lakshmi is said to enter the home through the purchase made on this day. Electronics, gold, silver, kitchen appliances, vehicles — everything counts.

**What people buy on Dhanteras:**

- **Gold & Silver** — coins, jewellery, utensils
- **Electronics** — laptops, phones, air fryers, smart TVs
- **Home Appliances** — mixer grinders, water purifiers, washing machines
- **Cookware** — stainless steel, copper vessels

**Deals to watch:** Both [Amazon coupon codes](/stores/amazon-coupon-code) and [Flipkart coupon codes](/stores/flipkart-coupon-code) are expected to run Dhanteras-specific offers on electronics and home appliances. Bank cashback offers on HDFC, SBI, and ICICI cards tend to stack well on these days — check both platforms before buying.

For home décor and appliances: [Pepperfry coupon codes](/stores/pepperfry-coupon-code) often run festival deals that bring furniture and home goods into a friendlier price bracket.

> **Pro tip:** The 48 hours before Dhanteras see significant price movement. Add items to cart a week early to track price drops.

## FAQ

**Q: When is Diwali 2026 in India?**
Diwali 2026 is on Sunday, 8 November. The five-day celebration runs from Dhanteras on November 6 to Bhai Dooj on November 11.

**Q: When is Dhanteras 2026?**
Dhanteras 2026 falls on Friday, 6 November. It is considered the most auspicious day to purchase gold, silver, and electronics.

**Q: What are the best things to buy in the Diwali sale 2026?**
Electronics, fashion, home décor, beauty products, and gifting sets see the strongest discounts. Both Amazon and Flipkart run dedicated Diwali sales in early November. Check active coupon codes on CouponsCrew before checkout.

**Q: Is Black Friday celebrated in India in 2026?**
Yes — Black Friday on 27 November is now a major sale event in India across Amazon, Flipkart, international fashion brands, and web hosting platforms. Tech and international brands offer some of the year's best prices.

**Q: When is Cyber Monday 2026?**
Cyber Monday 2026 is on Monday, 30 November. It is the best day to buy web hosting, software subscriptions, and digital tools — often cheaper than Black Friday for these categories.

**Q: What festivals fall in November 2026 in India?**
November 2026 has Dhanteras (6 Nov), Choti Diwali (7 Nov), Diwali (8 Nov), Govardhan Puja (10 Nov), Bhai Dooj (11 Nov), Chhath Puja (13–16 Nov), Dev Uthani Ekadashi (20 Nov), Tulsi Vivah (21 Nov), Guru Nanak Jayanti (24 Nov), Black Friday (27 Nov), and Cyber Monday (30 Nov).
