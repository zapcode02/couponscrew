## Three Things People Get Wrong About Wedding Shopping Sales

**Waiting for a "better deal" later.** Sale prices during the Grand Wedding Sale are typically the floor. Post-sale, prices return to full or near-full. The exception is clearance sales in January — but by then, the selection is whatever wasn't bought.

**Buying only for one function.** A lehenga worn once to one wedding is a poor return on investment. Buy styles that can be restyled — a different blouse, a plain statement neckpiece, a different dupatta draping. One outfit, three looks.

**Skipping the men's section.** Men's wedding shopping is usually an afterthought until three days before the function. The sherwani sizes go first. Shop now, return if plans change.
