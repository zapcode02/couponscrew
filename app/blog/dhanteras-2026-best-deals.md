## 🪙 Gold & Silver — The Classic Dhanteras Buy

### *"It's not spending. It's inviting prosperity home."*

Buying gold or silver on Dhanteras is the oldest tradition in the book. The belief: gold and silver are Goddess Lakshmi's metals. Bringing them home on Dhanteras means inviting her in.

And it doesn't have to be a heavy investment. A small gold coin, a silver Lakshmi-Ganesha idol, a set of silver spoons — the tradition is about the act of buying, not the amount.

**What to look for:**
- **Gold coins** — 24-karat or 22-karat, BIS hallmarked
- **Silver coins** — Lakshmi, Ganesha, or Lakshmi-Ganesha combo
- **Silver utensils** — bowls, spoons, glasses — functional and auspicious
- **Brass and copper vessels** — considered deeply auspicious, more affordable than silver

> *"The size of the coin doesn't matter. The tradition says buying something of value matters."*

**Where to find the deals:**
[Amazon coupon codes](/stores/amazon-coupon-code) — gold and silver coins from certified sellers, with bank cashback offers stacking on top. Compare prices across sellers and check for BIS hallmark certification before buying.
