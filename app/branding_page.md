## ✅ Usage Guidelines Section

**Section Heading:** Usage Guidelines

**Section subheading:** Consistency is how trust is built.

**Copy:**

For placements smaller than 120px wide, use the CouponsCrew icon mark rather than the full wordmark. This preserves legibility and brand clarity at small sizes — a compressed wordmark loses definition and reflects poorly on both the brand and the publisher using it.

When placing the logo on photography or textured backgrounds, always use the white or dark logo variant, and ensure sufficient clear space around all sides equal to the height of the "C" in CouponsCrew.

## ✅ Do's and Don'ts Section

**Section Heading:** Do's and Don'ts

**Section subheading:** How you use our brand matters — to us and to every shopper who trusts it.

---

### ✅ Please Do

- Use only the logo variants provided on this page
- Use the CouponsCrew icon mark when space is tight or the wordmark cannot be reproduced clearly
- Include the tagline **"Every Deal. Verified."** wherever the logo is published, except:
  1. Print materials where the tagline reduces below readable size
  2. Brand collaboration creatives where only the logo is required
  3. Tab/favicon representations of the brand
- Maintain the original aspect ratio of every logo file at all times
- Use the approved color combinations defined in the Brand Colors section above

---

### ❌ Please Don't

- Rotate, flip, or alter the direction of the logo
- Surround the logo with competing visual elements that reduce contrast or legibility
- Change any color in the logo outside the combinations defined on this page
- Use outdated or unofficial versions of the CouponsCrew logo
- Publish CouponsCrew brand assets on design portfolio platforms (Dribbble, Behance, etc.) without written permission from the CouponsCrew team
- Apply filters, gradients, drop shadows, or watermarks over the logo — partially or fully
- Stretch, squash, or distort the logo in any dimension
