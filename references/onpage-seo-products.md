# On-Page SEO Rules — Product Pages

> This file is self-contained. Read it fully before editing any product page.
> When keywords + a target page are provided, apply these rules to that page's title, meta description, body copy, headings, FAQ, images, and internal links — without rewriting or restructuring existing sentences. Insert keywords into natural gaps in the existing language. If no natural gap exists in a section, rephrase that one sentence minimally — never rewrite whole paragraphs to force a fit.

> **URL note:** Existing product URLs are NOT to be modified once published. This file governs keywords in title, meta, body, and other on-page elements only — not the slug of an already-live page.

---

## How This File Is Used

You will be given two things:
1. A keyword set — one Primary keyword + three Secondary keywords, chosen per product (see framework below)
2. A target product page

Apply the rules below to that page only. Do not touch other pages. Do not change tone, voice, or existing sentence structure beyond what's needed to fit a keyword naturally. Do not change the URL slug.

---

## Keyword Selection Framework — Choose Per Product

Product pages don't reuse the same 4 keywords across pages like store pages do (store pages all share "coupon code / promo code / discount code" pattern). Every product gets its own set, chosen by role:

| Role | Captures | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary** | Brand + core category | Exact-match relevance — this page = this product. Should mirror the title/H1. |
| **Secondary 1 — Audience/Variant** | Who it's for | Generic, non-branded searches — bigger top-of-funnel reach |
| **Secondary 2 — Attribute/Style** | Shape, color, material, design detail | Long-tail, high-relevance searchers |
| **Secondary 3 — Buying Intent** | Pack size, "buy online," price-angle phrasing | Lower volume, closest to purchase, highest conversion value |

**Worked example — Acnos Premium Girl's Heart Shape Bangle Watch, Pack of 2:**
```
Primary:      acnos bangle watch
Secondary 1:  girls bangle watch
Secondary 2:  heart shape watch
Secondary 3:  bangle watch pack of 2
```

**Note:** Secondary 1 and Secondary 3 are typically non-branded, so this page will also compete with other brands' listings on those terms — that's expected, not a problem.

**Slug reference for future new products** (not applied to existing pages): brand + category + ONE differentiator (whichever of audience/attribute is the stronger search term for that product type) + pack/qty if relevant. Never stack more than one differentiator into the slug.

## Final Keyword Density Targets

| Keyword | Total count (full page) |
|---|---|
| Primary | **5** |
| Secondary 1 | **3** |
| Secondary 2 | **3** |
| Secondary 3 | **2** |

**Total keyword instances across the page: 13.**
At a ~1,000–1,200 word body this is roughly 1.1–1.3% density — well inside safe range (spam risk generally starts above ~2-3%). Do not exceed these counts. One under is acceptable; one over is not.

"Full page" = title + meta description + body copy + FAQ questions/answers, combined. (Slug excluded — not modified per this ruleset.)

## Designated Placement Map

Every keyword mention has an assigned home. Do not place a keyword "wherever it fits."

### Primary Keyword — 5 total
| # | Slot |
|---|---|
| 1 | Title tag / H1 (same instance — do not count separately) — first 3 words |
| 2 | Meta description — first clause |
| 3 | First 100 words of body copy — opening sentence of the main product description |
| 4 | One H2 or H3 heading, phrased naturally |
| 5 | One more natural mention in body — Specifications or Product Highlights section |
| — | Stop at 5 — no additional mentions |

### Secondary 1 (Audience/Variant) — 3 total
| # | Slot |
|---|---|
| 1 | Meta description |
| 2 | "Who It's For" / product description opening — body |
| 3 | One FAQ answer, OR "Why Choose This" section — pick whichever has a natural gap |

### Secondary 2 (Attribute/Style) — 3 total
| # | Slot |
|---|---|
| 1 | Meta description |
| 2 | "Features & Specifications" section — body |
| 3 | One more natural mention in the intro or closing paragraph |

### Secondary 3 (Buying Intent) — 2 total
| # | Slot |
|---|---|
| 1 | Meta description |
| 2 | "Pack & Quantity Details" section, OR closing CTA paragraph near the Buy Now / Check Price link |

**Rule: minimum one full sentence gap between any two keyword mentions.** Never stack keywords in adjacent sentences.

## Meta Title Formula

```
[Primary Keyword] — [Key Feature/Benefit] | CouponsCrew
```

- Primary keyword = first words of the title, no exceptions
- One standout feature or benefit visible (pack size, standout attribute, or price angle)
- Under 60 characters total
- `| CouponsCrew` always at the end

**Example:**
`Acnos Bangle Watch — Heart Shape, Pack of 2 | CouponsCrew`

---

## Meta Description Formula

```
Shop the [Primary] online – a [Secondary 2] designed for [Secondary 1]. Comes as a [Secondary 3]. Best price & deals on CouponsCrew.
```

- 148–162 characters total
- Primary keyword in the first clause
- All 3 secondary keywords appear once each, naturally
- Must stand alone — don't write it as a continuation of the title sentence

**Example:**
`Shop the Acnos bangle watch online – a heart shape watch designed for girls bangle watch fans. Comes as a bangle watch pack of 2. Best price & deals on CouponsCrew.`

*(Adjust phrasing for natural flow once real keywords are substituted — the formula is a skeleton, not a script.)*

## The 5 Mandatory Slots — Every Product Page

### 1. Title Tag
Primary keyword at the start. 50–60 characters. Key feature visible.

### 2. First 100 Words of Body Copy
Primary keyword present. Direct answer up front — what the product is, who it's for, standout feature.

### 3. At Least One H2 Heading
Carries a secondary keyword or a question-form query.
Example: `Acnos Bangle Watch — Features & Specifications` / `Is This Bangle Watch Good for Daily Wear?`

### 4. Image Alt Text
Describes the image naturally; keyword only if the image actually depicts the subject (product shot, not a generic icon). Decorative/icon images get `alt=""`. Does **not** count toward the 13-instance keyword total.

### 5. Meta Description
148–162 characters, all 4 keywords present, key feature/benefit visible.

*(URL slug is excluded from this list — not modified per page.)*

## FAQ Section Rules

- 4–8 questions per page, each phrased as a real search query
- At least one question contains the primary keyword naturally
- One question/answer can carry a secondary keyword — but only if that secondary hasn't already hit its target count elsewhere; use a synonym instead if it has
- Answers: 2–4 sentences, one keyword maximum per answer — never stuff primary and secondary into the same answer

---

## Internal Links

- 3–5 internal links per page
- Descriptive, keyword-forward anchor text only — never "click here" / "read more"
- Correct: `girls bangle watch collection`, `heart shape watch styles`
- Anchor text does not count toward the 13-instance keyword total

---

## Outbound Affiliate Link (Amazon)

- The primary "Buy Now" / "Check Price on Amazon" link is a required conversion element on every product page — not optional
- Use `rel="nofollow sponsored"` and `target="_blank"` on the affiliate link, per Amazon Associates and Google guidelines for paid/affiliate links
- Keep this CTA visible above the fold, and repeat it at least once more near the bottom of the page
- This link does not count toward the "external links" quota below — it's a separate, mandatory element

## External Links (Non-Affiliate)

- 1–2 links to authoritative sources where relevant (brand's official site, a buying-guide or comparison resource) — lighter than store pages since the affiliate link is the dominant outbound element here
- Open in new tab, `rel="noopener"`

---

## Images

- Alt text on every image; keyword only where the image depicts the subject; `alt=""` for decorative/icon images
- Descriptive filenames, hyphenated (e.g. `acnos-heart-shape-bangle-watch.webp`)
- WebP format, compressed under 200KB
- Width/height attributes specified to prevent layout shift
- Lazy loading for below-fold images
- At least one clear product image above the fold, uncompressed enough to show detail

---

## Freshness Signal

- Display a visible "Price last checked: [date]" or "Availability updated: [date]" element, since product price/stock changes more often than store coupon codes
- Update this whenever price or availability is actually re-verified — don't leave a stale date live

---

## Copy Quality Rules

- Readability: 8th–10th grade level
- Active voice preferred
- Short paragraphs: 1–4 sentences
- Bullets/numbered lists for specifications and features — this is where lists matter most on a product page
- Length should roughly match top-3 competitor product pages for the same category, within ~20%
- Bold key phrases sparingly — not every keyword instance

---

## Headings Hierarchy

- Exactly one H1 per page, contains the primary keyword
- H2 → major sections (Features & Specifications, Why Choose This, Pack Details, FAQs)
- H3 → subsections, more specific/long-tail phrasing
- Never skip a level
- Not every heading needs a keyword — some should simply describe the section plainly

---

## Schema Markup (Correct Types for Product Pages)

Product pages use different schema than store/coupon pages:

- `Product` — name, image, brand, description
- `Offer` (nested in Product) — price, availability, `priceCurrency`, and the affiliate URL
- `AggregateRating` / `Review` — only if you have real rating/review data; never fabricate ratings or review counts
- `FAQPage` — wherever an FAQ section exists
- `BreadcrumbList` — every page

**Do not use `LocalBusiness` schema** — same rule as store pages, CouponsCrew is not a local business anywhere on the site.

---

## What NOT to Do

- Do not exceed the count for any keyword (Primary 5 / Sec1 3 / Sec2 3 / Sec3 2). One under is fine, one over is not.
- Do not place two keyword mentions in adjacent sentences.
- Do not repeat the exact same secondary keyword phrase in two adjacent paragraphs — use a synonym once a slot is filled nearby.
- Do not keyword-stuff the FAQ — one keyword per question, one per answer.
- Do not rewrite existing sentences wholesale to fit a keyword — find the natural gap, insert cleanly.
- Do not modify the URL slug of a published product page.
- Do not put keyword alt text on purely decorative/icon images — use `alt=""`.
- Do not fabricate ratings, review counts, or availability status in schema or body copy.
- Do not use `LocalBusiness` schema anywhere on this site.
